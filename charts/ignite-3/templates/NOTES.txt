{{- if .Values.autoscaling.create }}

WARNING: autoscaling.create is enabled. Autoscaling is NOT recommended for Ignite 3 clusters:
  - The CMG/metastorage group is fixed at initialization; scaling down below its majority makes the cluster unavailable.
  - Scaling down removes nodes without draining them; partitions with fewer replicas than removed nodes become lost or unavailable.
  - Every `helm upgrade` resets the replicas to replicaCount, overriding the HPA.
