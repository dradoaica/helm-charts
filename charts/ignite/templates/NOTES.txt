{{- if .Values.autoscaling.create }}

WARNING: autoscaling.create is enabled. Autoscaling is NOT recommended for Ignite clusters:
  - With persistence, new pods hold no data until they are added to the baseline topology.
  - Scaling down removes nodes without draining them; partitions without backups become lost or unavailable.
  - Every `helm upgrade` resets the replicas to replicaCount, overriding the HPA.
